How to read a permit history

The Township lists permits issued since January 1, 2011 and removes them 30 days after final inspection approval. A folder may cover several civic addresses. We count it once and keep all listed addresses.

Each complete snapshot establishes whether a folder was listed at that check. A disappearance falls between two checks. Its exact final inspection date is unknown. The first snapshot establishes a baseline for future changes. Development applications above use separate municipal identifiers.

What “changed” means

Changes are differences observed between this tool’s snapshots, beginning on the date shown above. An older application is not labelled new just because it appears in the first snapshot. A record disappearing from a source is retained and marked “no longer listed”; that does not establish cancellation or completion. Original statuses remain visible alongside broad filter groups.

Refreshes, privacy and saved applications

A scheduled GitHub workflow checks the sources daily. A failed or incomplete refresh keeps the previous snapshot and deployment. Retrieval time is not the date a proposal changed. Official source pages take precedence.

Searching stays in your browser. Only IDs you explicitly save are stored locally under coolcode:langleychanges:watchlist. CoolCode counts page opens by tool ID. Your searches and watchlist stay out of that count. OpenStreetMap receives tile requests for the map area; source links open the municipality’s website.

Application descriptions and links are from the public municipal sources. Personal applicant contact details are not copied into this snapshot.
