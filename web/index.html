Two sources, different coverage

The Township source covers mapped development applications, including projects retained for up to a year after completion. The City source contains the applications featured on its public portal; it says the monthly reports provide the full active list. This app does not claim to include every application in both municipalities.

What “changed” means

Changes are differences observed between this tool’s snapshots, beginning on the date shown above. An older application is not labelled new just because it appears in the first snapshot. A record disappearing from a source is retained and marked “no longer listed”; that does not establish cancellation or completion. Original statuses remain visible alongside broad filter groups.

Refreshes, privacy and saved applications

A scheduled GitHub workflow checks the sources daily. A failed or incomplete refresh keeps the previous snapshot and deployment. Retrieval time is not the date a proposal changed. Official source pages take precedence.

Searching stays in your browser. Only IDs you explicitly save are stored locally under coolcode:langleychanges:watchlist. No accounts, email alerts or analytics. OpenStreetMap receives tile requests for the map area; source links open the municipality’s website.

Application descriptions and links are from the public municipal sources. Personal applicant contact details are not copied into this snapshot.
